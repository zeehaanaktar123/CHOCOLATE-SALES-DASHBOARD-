## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI sales analytics dashboard** developed to analyze multi-country chocolate sales performance and identify trends across time, products, and countries.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from **data cleaning and transformation in Excel Power Query** to **relational analysis using MySQL** and interactive visualization in **Power BI**.

The dashboard is designed to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support hypothetical **inventory planning, product performance evaluation, and marketing decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard focuses on answering key business questions:

* How are sales changing over time?
* Which products are the best performers?
* Which countries contribute the most to sales?
* How does product performance vary across countries?
* Which products or markets may require further business attention?
* What insights can support inventory and marketing decisions?

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides interactive analysis of sales performance.

### Key Dashboard Areas

#### 📅 Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzes sales performance over time using date-based analysis to identify trends and changes in sales.

#### 🍫 Product Performance

Identifies top-performing products and compares product-level sales performance.

#### 🌍 Country Performance

Analyzes sales across countries to identify stronger and weaker-performing markets.

#### 🔝 Top Products

Highlights the top-performing products based on sales performance.
